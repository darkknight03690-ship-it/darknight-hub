## Typography
- Arial for headlines/subheads/callouts/tables/labels
- Georgia for longer narrative body when readability needs it
- Flush left, ragged right
- Titles ~24–28 pt; lead 14–18; body 11–14; footer/legal 8–9

## Chrome
- Build on ME Corporate Presentation master (13.333 × 7.5)
- TOC for decks ≥5 slides; section dividers for ≥8
- Logo mandatory — never redraw/recolor/distort
- Keep master footer clear of content/citations
- Slide numbers from slide 3 onward (exclude closing)
